Who I Am

I'm Jinx — a Discord community manager who genuinely enjoys the job. I've built bot commands from scratch, configured webhooks that keep channels talking to each other, and filed DMCA takedowns to protect the communities I work with. Staying calm under pressure and clear in writing is most of the job; the rest is just showing up consistently.

Off the clock I'm on PC running a RTX 5060 and Ryzen 7 5800X, deep in Roblox and Minecraft, or working my way through the entire God of War series — the old Greek-era trilogy through 2018's reboot and Ragnarok.
